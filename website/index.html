How it works

From first lesson to interview day

  1. 1

    Learn

    Short, clear lessons explain the ideas behind each civics question, from the Constitution to the branches of government to key moments in American history.

  2. 2

    Practice

    Answer questions in the same question-and-answer style as the civics test, with a friendly explanation after every answer, right or wrong.

  3. 3

    Feel ready

    Keep practicing the questions you find hardest until the answers come naturally, so you can walk into your naturalization interview prepared.

Our mission

Every future citizen deserves a clear path.

Hundreds of thousands of people become U.S. citizens every year. Preparing for the civics test can feel overwhelming, especially when you're balancing work, family, and a new language. Pathways USA is a nonprofit on a mission to give everyone on that journey a patient, high-quality tutor, no matter their background.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When will Pathways be available?

We're putting the finishing touches on the app now. Sign up and we'll email you as soon as it's on the App Store.

Will there be an Android version?

We're launching on the App Store first. If you use Android, choose it in the sign-up form so we know how many people are waiting.

Who is Pathways for?

Anyone preparing for the civics portion of the U.S. naturalization interview, and the teachers, families, and organizations who support them.

Is Pathways affiliated with USCIS or the government?

No. Pathways USA is an independent nonprofit. Pathways is a study tool, not legal advice. For official information about naturalization, visit uscis.gov/citizenship.

How can I help?

Share this page with someone preparing for citizenship, or with a teacher or organization that supports immigrants. If you'd like to partner with us, sign up as a community organization and we'll be in touch.
